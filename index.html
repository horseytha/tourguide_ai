How recommendations work

NLTK VADER + K-Means

Wanderly ranks stays in the selected city using your interests, traveler type, and available review signals:

  • Interest matching: Matches your selected preferences to stay descriptions and tags.
  • Review signals: Combines guest ratings and sentiment scores to help rank suggestions.
  • Traveler fit: Uses traveler-type signals from the sample dataset to tune recommendations.
  • Route planning: Groups suggested stops across trip days and orders them by proximity.
About the suggestions: Recommendations come from the sample stays included with this project. The map and route are planning aids, not live availability or transit directions.
